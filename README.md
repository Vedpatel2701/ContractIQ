# ContractIQ

> AI-Powered Legal Documentation & Contract Intelligence Platform

An intelligent platform inspired by the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) **AI-Powered Legal Documentation Assistant**, extended with advanced contract intelligence features to simplify legal document analysis and contract management.

---

## 📖 Overview

LegalDocAI is designed to help organizations and individuals efficiently understand, analyze, and manage legal documents such as contracts, agreements, and affidavits. The platform leverages Artificial Intelligence (AI), Natural Language Processing (NLP), Optical Character Recognition (OCR), and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to automatically extract important information, explain legal clauses in simple language, identify potential risks, and provide intelligent contract management features.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📄 Upload legal documents (PDF, DOCX, Images)
- 🔍 OCR support for scanned documents
- 🤖 AI-powered legal document analysis
- 📝 Automatic contract summarization
- 📌 Clause and key information extraction
- ⚠️ Contract risk analysis
- 💬 AI chatbot for legal document queries
- 📅 Smart reminders for renewals and deadlines
- 🔎 Semantic search across uploaded contracts
- 📊 Contract management dashboard
